Core capabilities

Privacy protection built into every input layer

ShieldAI combines detection, scoring, masking, and policy enforcement to keep sensitive data out of unsafe AI interactions.

🧠
Detection

PII Detection

Identifies names, addresses, emails, IDs, and other sensitive data embedded in user prompts.

📊
Analytics

Risk Scoring

Scores every prompt by sensitivity and potential impact to prioritize remediation and monitoring.

🎭
Protection

Smart Masking

Replaces sensitive details with placeholders to preserve context while protecting confidential information.

🛡️
Governance

Policy Engine

Applies enterprise rules for when data should be blocked, masked, or allowed based on compliance needs.

✨
Sanitization

Prompt Sanitization

Removes harmful or unintended content and keeps prompts safe for external AI model consumption.

🚧
Defense

AI Protection

Stops privacy leaks before they reach LLM providers, third-party tools, or public AI interfaces.
