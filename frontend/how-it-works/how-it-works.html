How it works

How ShieldAI protects your prompts before they leave your system

ShieldAI works as a privacy gate between user input and external AI models. It inspects, classifies, and sanitizes risky content before it reaches any LLM.

01

User Prompt

A user enters sensitive information such as a name, email, API key, or confidential business data into an AI workflow.

↓
02

ShieldAI

The system receives the input and runs a detection layer to identify risky patterns, personally identifiable information, and policy violations.

↓
03

Detection

Pattern checks, context analysis, and rule logic identify PII, secrets, health data, passwords, and confidential records.

↓
04

Risk Scoring

Every prompt is assigned a risk score based on sensitivity and impact so security teams can quickly prioritize incidents.

↓
05

Protection

The policy engine decides whether to mask, redact, block, or allow the content according to company rules and compliance requirements.

↓
06

Safe Prompt

Risky content is converted to a sanitized version, such as replacing names or emails with placeholders while preserving the prompt structure.

↓
07

External AI

Only the safe prompt reaches the LLM, keeping private information out of AI training and third-party exposure.
