Policy matrix

Default protection rules for sensitive data

ShieldAI applies a clear policy action for each type of detected content to reduce exposure and maintain compliance.

EMAIL MASK

Email addresses are replaced with a neutral placeholder while preserving message structure.

PHONE MASK

Phone numbers are hidden during AI processing to prevent contact leakage or social engineering risks.

NAME MASK

Personal names are sanitized before leaving the organization to maintain privacy.

PASSWORD BLOCK

Passwords are blocked immediately because they represent direct account compromise risk.

API KEY BLOCK

API keys and tokens are rejected to prevent unauthorized access to backend services.

FINANCIAL REDACT

Cards, account numbers, and banking data are redacted before being sent to external models.

HEALTH MASK

Medical or health-related data is masked to support compliance with privacy regulations.

CONFIDENTIAL BLOCK

Highly sensitive or internal-only information is prevented from leaving secure systems.
