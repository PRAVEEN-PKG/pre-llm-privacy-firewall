Security & Privacy Enforcement Policies

ShieldAI evaluates incoming tokens and applies deterministic policy actions (Mask, Redact, or Block) based on data sensitivity and compliance mandates.

EMAIL RULE-PII-01
MASK

Email addresses are intercepted and substituted with a structure-preserving placeholder (e.g. [EMAIL_1]) preventing user contact leak.

• Low Impact on Context Active in Strict & Balanced
PHONE RULE-PII-02
MASK

International and domestic phone numbers are sanitized before external transmission, mitigating phishing and social engineering vectors.

• Low Impact on Context Active in Strict & Balanced
NAME RULE-PII-03
MASK

Personal names and customer identifiers are anonymized with sequential tokens to protect personal identity and GDPR compliance.

• Medium Impact Active in Strict Mode
REGISTRATION NUMBER RULE-PII-04
REDACT

Vehicle plates and labeled registration identifiers are replaced with a fixed placeholder before prompt transmission.

• Sensitive Identifier Enforced in All Policies
PASSWORD RULE-SEC-01
BLOCK

Passwords, secret pins, and authentication strings are dropped immediately with prompt rejection to prevent accidental credential leakage into AI weights.

• Critical Security Threat Enforced in All Policies
API KEY RULE-SEC-02
BLOCK

High-entropy developer secrets (OpenAI, AWS, GitHub, Stripe tokens) trigger a firewall tripwire, preventing unauthorized infrastructure takeover.

• Critical Security Threat Enforced in All Policies
FINANCIAL RULE-PCI-01
REDACT

Credit card numbers (Luhn validated), IBANs, and bank account numbers are scrubbed with cryptographic redaction ensuring PCI-DSS compliance.

• High Regulatory Risk Active in Strict & Balanced
HEALTH RULE-HIPAA-01
MASK

Protected Health Information (PHI) including diagnosis codes and patient records are de-identified under HIPAA Safe Harbor rules.

• High Privacy Impact Active in Strict Mode
CONFIDENTIAL RULE-CORP-01
BLOCK

Proprietary corporate codebases, internal trade secrets, and labeled NDA documents are stopped before departing the organizational boundary.

• Critical Enterprise Risk Enforced in Strict
